About Amy Yang

Amy Yang currently serves as Chief Technical Advisor at EdgeConNex, specializing in global digital infrastructure, hyperscale data centers, AI computing environments, and critical infrastructure technology strategy. With extensive professional experience in technology consulting, data center operations, data engineering, and IT strategy, she is committed to bridging advanced technologies with real-world business needs, providing technical insights and strategic guidance to support the development of next-generation digital infrastructure.

Since assuming her role as Chief Technical Advisor at EdgeConNex in February 2023, Amy has been involved in the planning and evaluation of data center technology strategies, with a particular focus on power systems, cooling technologies, network architecture, and high-density computing infrastructure. She closely follows emerging technologies, including liquid cooling, cold-plate cooling, immersion cooling, and edge computing. She advises clients and joint venture partners throughout the data center planning process, from evaluating technical solutions to supporting commercial contract negotiations. Her work emphasizes critical infrastructure reliability, compliance with industry standards, operational efficiency, and total cost of ownership (TCO) optimization, with the goal of balancing technological advancement and long-term business value.

"Exceptional digital infrastructure requires more than cutting-edge technology. It demands reliable engineering practices, long-term strategic vision, and the ability to turn innovation into meaningful, real-world value."
— Amy Yang

Before becoming Chief Technical Advisor, Amy served as Data Center Operations Manager at EdgeConNex from February 2020 to January 2023, overseeing the day-to-day operations and management of critical data center facilities. In this role, she focused on maintaining continuous and reliable data center operations, meeting customer service-level agreements (SLAs), and supporting the high-availability infrastructure requirements of cloud computing and content service customers. This experience strengthened her expertise in critical facilities management, business continuity, operational risk management, and customer service, providing a strong foundation for her subsequent work in technology strategy consulting.

Prior to joining EdgeConNex, Amy worked as a Data Engineer at Elastic in the San Francisco Bay Area from February 2018 to May 2019. She contributed to enterprise IT development, data systems planning, and technology implementation, helping formulate IT solutions aligned with business requirements while supporting the development, operation, and optimization of enterprise systems. Her responsibilities encompassed IT risk management, information security, data technology applications, and cross-functional technical collaboration. Through this work, she helped improve system reliability, operational efficiency, and technology management capabilities.

From November 2015 to May 2017, Amy served as a Data Consultant at SequoiaDB Ltd. in Shenzhen, China, providing enterprise clients with data analysis, technical solution design, and implementation consulting services. Working closely with client engineering teams, she helped identify complex data management challenges and develop practical solutions that translated data technologies into measurable business value. This experience further strengthened her understanding of enterprise databases, data architecture, and digital transformation.
